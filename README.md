### Hi, I'm Jose 👋

I build native apps, games and interactive web projects — mostly in **Swift**, **Godot** and **JavaScript**. I'm a high school developer in the Seattle area, I compete in FBLA, TSA and DECA, and I'm headed toward computer science and data science.

#### Featured work

**[Prism](https://github.com/JoseVGerald/prism)** — a native YouTube Music client for macOS and iPhone. SwiftUI with Liquid Glass, DJ-style beat-matched transitions, synced lyrics, listening stats and a MacBook notch island. About 20k lines of Swift, built without an Xcode project.

**[Course Galaxy](https://github.com/JoseVGerald/course-galaxy)** — a 3D galaxy you fly through where every course is a planet, rendered in Blender and brought to life in three.js with custom shaders.

**My Little Buddy** — a financial-literacy virtual pet game in Godot 4 with an on-device AI assistant (Ollama), built with two teammates. 1st at FBLA Regionals and 6th at FBLA Nationals 2026.

**AI Nexus** — a gamified AI-literacy portal for high schoolers, in progress with my team for TSA Webmaster 2026–27.
